# Footer video: "One line, every game"

A 30-second background loop behind the footer. It's one continuous etched illustration on a single horizon line, drifting left to right through six game worlds and back to the start. It's ambient only: no sound, no controls, can't be clicked.

## The rules for every frame

- **Horizon line:** always at **62% of the frame height**, perfectly level, in every keyframe of every scene. This is what makes six clips read as one shot.
- **Camera:** a slow lateral drift, left to right, about 3% of frame width per second. No cuts, no shake, no handheld feel. Only scenes 5 and 6 add one gentle push or pull, and they finish back at the horizon framing.
- **Sky:** the top 45% stays mostly empty paper, because the footer links sit there. Keep detail below the line and low in the sky.
- **Style:** see the style block below. Paste it at the end of every prompt, image and video.
- **Accent:** orange appears only on the thing listed for each scene. Everything else is ink on paper.

### Style block (append to every prompt)

> Fine etched engraving illustration, copperplate line art with dense cross-hatching and stippling, single-colour ink in warm near-black (#2A1F14) on light warm yellow paper (#FBF1D3), subtle paper grain, vintage toile and banknote engraving feel, highly detailed but calm, wide cinematic panorama, perfectly level horizon line at 62% of frame height, empty paper sky in the upper half, no text, no logos, no watermark, only accent colour: small touches of bright orange (#FF6B00) where specified.

### Negative prompt (if the tool takes one)

> photo, 3D render, gradient sky, colour fill, blue ink, text, letters, logos, brand names, watermark, signature, frame border, vignette, lens flare, camera shake, motion blur smear, morphing faces, extra limbs

## Scene 1: The astronaut (0–5s)

**Start frame:** a grey, cratered lunar plain on the horizon line. In the near distance a large round moon-like planet hangs low, its edge just above the horizon at the left third. *This circle is where the loop lands from scene 6.* A small astronaut stands mid-left beside a sleek single-seat space jet parked on the right.

> A lone astronaut on a cratered moon surface, standing mid-left, a sleek small space jet parked on the right with its canopy open, a large pale planet low on the horizon at left, long soft shadows, engraved craters and dust. [style block] Accent: none yet.

**End frame:** the astronaut is in the cockpit and the jet is lifting off to the upper right. A long engraved exhaust trail curls upward from the ground and fans into a soft cloud-like plume. The flame is the only orange.

> The same moon plain, the space jet lifting off toward the upper right, a long curling exhaust trail rising from the ground and spreading into a soft billowing plume at the right edge, small orange flame at the engine. [style block]

**Motion prompt:**
> The astronaut hops in slow, low-gravity bounds toward the jet, little puffs of dust at each landing, climbs in, canopy closes, the jet lifts gently and arcs up and to the right, its exhaust trail curling into a billowing plume. Camera drifts slowly left to right, horizon stays level. Calm, weightless, no cuts.

## Scene 2: The skydiver (5–10s)

**Start frame:** the plume from scene 1 now reads as a low cloud bank sitting on the horizon. Far below the line, a patchwork of fields. High above, a tiny skydiver in freefall, arms spread.

> A vast sky over a low cloud bank resting on the horizon line, patchwork farmland faintly visible below, a tiny skydiver in freefall high in the upper right, arms and legs spread, engraved cloud forms. [style block] Accent: none.

**End frame:** the parachute has opened, a big rounded canopy in the left-centre, floating down toward the horizon. *The canopy dome is the hand-off shape.*

> The same sky, the skydiver now under a large open rounded parachute canopy, centred slightly left, drifting down toward the horizon, the canopy filling the middle of the frame. [style block]

**Motion prompt:**
> The skydiver falls in a smooth arc from upper right toward centre, flips gently, the parachute bursts open into a full rounded canopy and the descent slows to a gentle float. Clouds drift slowly. Camera drifts left to right, horizon level. Smooth, no cuts.

## Scene 3: The orange tree (10–15s)

**Start frame:** the canopy shape has become the rounded crown of a single orange tree standing on a gentle hill on the horizon. Bright orange fruits dot the crown, the only colour. A woman in a simple long dress walks in from the left with a woven basket.

> A single full orange tree with a perfectly rounded crown standing on a gentle hill on the horizon, bright orange fruits among engraved leaves, a woman in a simple long dress walking in from the left carrying a woven basket, quiet orchard landscape. [style block] Accent: the oranges.

**End frame:** the woman stands under the tree holding one orange up in her raised hand, against the open sky. *The orange is the hand-off shape.*

> The same tree, the woman standing beneath it holding one orange up in her raised hand against the sky, the orange clear and round, other fruits on the branches. [style block] Accent: the oranges.

**Motion prompt:**
> The woman walks calmly to the tree, reaches up, plucks an orange and lifts it toward the light, leaves sway slightly in a breeze. Camera drifts left to right, horizon level. Gentle, unhurried, no cuts.

## Scene 4: The aim-assist (15–20s)

**Start frame:** a long open shooting range on the horizon. A round target board on a wooden stand sits mid-right. In front of it floats a thin engraved crosshair reticle with circles, tick marks and four brackets, searching off-target.

> An open outdoor shooting range on the horizon, a round bullseye target on a simple wooden stand mid-right, a large thin engraved crosshair reticle with concentric circles, tick marks and four corner brackets floating left of the target, scanning. [style block] Accent: the bullseye centre.

**End frame:** the reticle has locked perfectly onto the bullseye, rings aligned, brackets closed in. *The concentric rings are the hand-off shape.*

> The same range, the crosshair reticle locked exactly onto the bullseye, its circles aligned with the target rings, brackets tightened around it, the orange bullseye centre glowing. [style block]

**Motion prompt:**
> The crosshair reticle sweeps smoothly across the range, slows, snaps onto the target, its rings tighten and the brackets close in with a small click-like settle, the bullseye centre glows orange. Camera drifts left to right, horizon level. Precise, smooth, no cuts.

## Scene 5: The racer (20–25s)

**Start frame:** a tight side close-up of a motorcycle helmet with its glossy visor, rider leaning forward. Streaks of passing landscape reflect in the visor. Its round outline echoes the target's rings.

> Extreme close-up side view of a racing motorcycle helmet with a glossy visor, rider leaning forward, streaks of a passing landscape reflected in the visor, engraved highlights. [style block] Accent: a thin orange stripe on the helmet.

**End frame:** the camera has pulled out to a wide side view. A racer on a sport motorbike is leaning low on a winding road that runs exactly along the horizon line, with speed lines engraved behind. The orange tail light is the accent.

> Wide side view of a rider on a sport motorbike leaning into a curve on a road that runs along the horizon line, engraved speed lines and a blurred landscape behind, orange tail light. [style block]

**Motion prompt:**
> Start tight on the helmet visor with reflections streaking past, then a smooth continuous pull-out revealing the rider and the motorbike racing along a road on the horizon, the bike leaning through a curve, light streaks across the visor. Ends in a wide shot with the horizon level. Fast feel, smooth camera, no cuts.

## Scene 6: The mobile gamer (25–30s)

**Start frame:** close-up of two hands holding a phone sideways, thumbs on the screen. The screen glows orange (the only colour) with an abstract shooting-game silhouette. Behind, through a window, the same horizon line runs across the room.

> Close-up of two hands holding a smartphone sideways playing a mobile shooting game, thumbs mid-press, the screen glowing orange with an abstract game silhouette, a window behind showing a distant horizon line at the same height, engraved room details. [style block] Accent: the screen glow.

**End frame:** a side-profile close-up of the player's head wearing over-ear headphones. The round ear cup fills the left third, positioned exactly where scene 1's moon sits. *That circle is the loop point.*

> Side-profile close-up of a young person's head wearing large over-ear headphones, the round ear cup large and placed at the left third of the frame, calm focused expression, engraved hair and fabric, the same distant horizon line behind. [style block]

**Motion prompt:**
> Quick precise thumb movements on the phone, small taps and swipes, the orange screen flickering with action, then the camera glides up and around to a side profile and slowly zooms toward the round ear cup of the headphones until it fills the left third of the frame. Horizon stays level in the background. Smooth, no cuts.

**Loop:** crossfade the last 8 frames of scene 6 into the first frame of scene 1, with the ear cup becoming the moon.

## Assembly and export

1. Generate all **12 keyframes** (start and end per scene) as stills first, at 1920×1080. Check that every end frame lines up with the next start frame: same shape, same position, same horizon.
2. Animate each scene **from its start frame to its end frame** (5s each) in a tool that accepts both frames.
3. In your editor, join the six clips with **6–10 frame crossfades** on the match shapes. Grade everything to the same paper colour (`#FBF1D3`) and ink (`#2A1F14`).
4. Export:

| File | Spec |
|---|---|
| `public/videos/footer/loop.mp4` | H.264, 1920×1080, 24fps, no audio, about 4–6 Mbps, **under 8 MB** |
| `public/videos/footer/loop.webm` | VP9, same size, a little smaller |
| `public/videos/footer/loop-mobile.mp4` | 1080×1350 centre crop, **under 4 MB** (optional) |
| `public/videos/footer/poster.webp` | First frame, quality 80 |

Why not a GIF: a 30s GIF at this size would be 40 MB or more, and its 256-colour limit breaks the fine hatching into bands. A muted, looping, autoplaying video looks exactly like a GIF on the page at a fraction of the size.

## How it sits in the footer (for the build)

- Full-width footer on paper colour `#FBF1D3`, with the video filling the bottom 60–65% and fading into the paper at its top edge.
- Logo, one line of copy and the link columns sit in the empty sky area above the horizon, in ink colour.
- The video is decorative: `muted autoplay loop playsinline`, no controls, `disablepictureinpicture`, `pointer-events: none`, `user-select: none`, `aria-hidden`. It only plays while the footer is on screen. Reduced-motion users see the poster frame.
