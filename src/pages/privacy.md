## 3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with PandaPay Africa, we may collect the following categories of information:

### A. Personal and Contact Information

- Name or display name.
- Phone number.
- Email address.
- Other contact details you provide.

### B. Transaction and Order Information

- Products purchased.
- Product denominations and regions.
- Order references and transaction history.
- Payment status and transaction confirmations.
- Refund and support records.

### C. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. We may receive transaction references, payment confirmations, payment status and limited payment-related information from those providers.

We do not intend to collect or store full card details, PINs, passwords or other sensitive payment authentication credentials unless a specific lawful and secure process requires it.

### D. WhatsApp and Communication Information

- Messages sent to our WhatsApp business channels.
- Order enquiries and customer support conversations.
- Information shared when reporting an issue.
- Communication preferences and relevant interaction records.

### E. Technical and Usage Information

When you access our website or digital services, we may collect information such as:

- IP address.
- Browser and device information.
- Operating system.
- Website activity and interaction data.
- Cookies and similar technologies, where used.
- Security and diagnostic information.

We collect only information reasonably necessary for our business purposes and legal obligations.

## 4. How We Collect Information

We may collect personal information:

- Directly from you when you place an order.

We may use personal information to:

- Process and confirm payments.
- Deliver digital products and services.
- Provide mobile game top-ups and VTU services.
- Respond to enquiries and complaints.
- Investigate failed, delayed or disputed transactions.
- Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised activity.
- Maintain platform security.
- Improve our website, products and customer experience.
- Send service-related notifications.
- Send marketing communications where legally permitted.
- Comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.
- Establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## 6. Lawful Basis for Processing

Where required by applicable law, we process personal information on one or more of the following lawful bases:

**Contract:** To fulfil orders and provide services requested by you.

**Legal obligation:** To comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

**Legitimate interests:** To protect our business, prevent fraud, secure our systems and improve our services, where these interests do not override your rights.

**Consent:** Where you have given consent for a specific processing activity, such as certain marketing communications or optional technologies.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it, subject to legal requirements and the consequences of withdrawal.

## 7. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

However, we may share information where reasonably necessary with:

**Payment providers:** To process payments, confirm transactions and investigate payment issues.

**Digital product suppliers:** To fulfil gift card, gaming and other digital product orders.

**Technology providers:** To host, maintain, secure and operate our website and systems.

**Messaging providers:** To operate customer communications and WhatsApp-related services.

**Professional advisers:** Including legal, accounting and compliance advisers.

**Authorities and regulators:** Where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

**Other parties:** Where necessary to protect our rights, customers, business or systems.

Third parties receiving personal information may process it under their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

## 8. International Transfers

Some suppliers, technology providers, payment processors or messaging services may operate outside Nigeria.

As a result, personal information may be transferred to or accessed from other countries.

Where international transfers occur, we will take steps required by applicable data protection law to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.

Retention periods may depend on:

- The nature of the information.
- The purpose for which it was collected.
- Transaction and accounting requirements.
- Legal and regulatory obligations.
- Fraud prevention and dispute resolution needs.
- The establishment or defence of legal claims.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete, anonymise or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable law.

## 10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, restricted permissions, secure service providers and appropriate operational safeguards.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should also protect their account credentials, payment details and digital redemption codes.
