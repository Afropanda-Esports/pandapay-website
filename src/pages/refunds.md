## 3. General Refund Principles

Refund requests are assessed based on the nature of the transaction, payment status, product type, delivery status and available evidence.

Because many of our products are digital and may be delivered or redeemed immediately, some transactions cannot be reversed once successfully fulfilled.

However, we will investigate genuine cases involving failed payments, duplicate charges, non-delivery, incorrect fulfilment or defective products.

Nothing in this Policy limits mandatory refund or consumer protection rights under applicable law.

## 4. Eligible Refund Situations

A refund or other appropriate resolution may be considered in the following situations:

### A. Payment Successful but Product Not Delivered

Where payment has been confirmed but the purchased product has not been delivered, we will investigate the transaction. If we are unable to fulfil the order, we may provide a refund or another mutually agreed resolution, subject to applicable law.

### B. Duplicate Payment

If a customer is charged more than once for the same order, the duplicate payment will be investigated. Where a duplicate charge is confirmed and the additional payment has not been used to fulfil another order, the excess amount will be refunded.

### C. Incorrect Product Delivered

Where PandaPay delivers a product that materially differs from the product confirmed in the customer's order, we will investigate and, where the error is confirmed, arrange an appropriate correction, replacement or refund.

### D. Invalid or Defective Digital Code

If a gift card or voucher supplied by PandaPay is genuinely invalid, previously redeemed before delivery or otherwise defective at the time of delivery, the customer may submit a complaint for investigation. A refund or replacement may be considered where the issue is verified and attributable to the supplied product or our fulfilment process.

### E. Failed Mobile Game Top-Up or VTU Transaction

Where a payment is successful but the requested top-up or VTU service is not completed, we will investigate with the relevant provider. If the transaction cannot be fulfilled, an appropriate refund or other resolution may be provided.

## 5. Non-Refundable or Restricted Situations

Subject to applicable law, a refund may not be available in the following circumstances:

- A valid digital gift card or voucher has already been successfully delivered and is usable.
- A gift card or voucher has been redeemed after delivery.
- A customer selected the wrong country, region, denomination or product.
- A customer provided an incorrect gaming account ID, user ID, phone number or other required information and the transaction was successfully completed.
- A customer is unable to redeem a product because their account is registered in an unsupported region.
- A customer changes their mind after a digital product has been successfully delivered, where cancellation rights do not apply.
- A customer has shared a delivered code with another person or exposed it to unauthorised access.
- A product is used in a way that violates the issuer's or platform's terms.

These restrictions do not apply where the product is defective, incorrectly supplied or otherwise eligible for a remedy under applicable law.

## 6. Wrong Product or Region Selection

Customers are responsible for checking the product details before completing payment.

This includes:

- Gift card region and country.
- Currency and denomination.
- Gaming platform compatibility.
- Account region.
- Mobile game account details.
- Network and phone number for VTU services.

Where an order has not yet been processed, we may be able to correct or cancel it. Where a region-specific digital product has already been delivered or a top-up has been successfully processed, cancellation or refund may not be possible unless required by law or otherwise approved by PandaPay.

## 7. Cancellation Requests

Customers who wish to cancel an order should contact our support team as soon as possible.

Cancellation may be possible where:

- Payment has not yet been completed.
- The order has not yet entered processing.
- The product has not been issued or delivered.
- The relevant supplier or service provider permits cancellation.

Once a digital product has been issued, delivered or successfully processed, cancellation may no longer be possible.

We will assess cancellation requests individually and in accordance with applicable law.

## 8. Invalid Gift Card and Voucher Complaints

Customers reporting an invalid or defective gift card should contact support promptly.

To help us investigate, we may request:

- Order reference or transaction ID.
- Product type and denomination.
- Country or region of the gift card.
- Date and approximate time of attempted redemption.
- A screenshot or error message from the official redemption platform.
- Other reasonable supporting information.

Customers should not publish or send full gift card codes through public channels. Where necessary, we may request secure submission of relevant code information through an appropriate support channel.

We may contact the supplier or relevant platform to verify the complaint.

A complaint does not automatically establish that a product was defective. Refunds or replacements will be considered after reasonable investigation.

## 10. Refund Review and Processing

Once a refund request is received, we will review the transaction and may contact the payment provider, supplier or relevant service provider.

We may request further information where necessary.

If a refund is approved, it will generally be returned through the original payment method where reasonably possible. The time taken for funds to appear in a customer's account may depend on the payment provider, bank or financial institution.

We will communicate the outcome of the request through the contact details supplied by the customer.

## 11. Refund Amount and Partial Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the amount will reflect the eligible value of the affected transaction.

In certain cases, a partial refund may be appropriate where only part of an order was unsuccessful or incorrectly fulfilled.

Any deductions or adjustments will be made only where permitted by applicable law and appropriately explained to the customer.

## 12. Payment Reversals and Chargebacks

Customers are encouraged to contact PandaPay support first where a transaction issue arises.

We will make reasonable efforts to investigate and resolve genuine payment disputes.

Unauthorised chargebacks or payment reversals may affect the processing of an order and may require additional verification.

Nothing in this section prevents a customer from exercising any lawful rights available through their bank, payment provider or relevant authority.

## 13. Promotional Vouchers and Discount Codes

Promotional vouchers, discount codes and rewards may be subject to specific conditions.

Unless applicable law or the offer terms require otherwise:

- Promotional vouchers are not redeemable for cash.
- Vouchers may be limited to specified products or regions.
- Vouchers may have an expiry date.
- Unused promotional value may not be refundable.
- A promotional benefit may not be reinstated after successful redemption.

Where a refund is approved for an order involving a promotional voucher, the refund treatment will depend on the offer terms and applicable law.

## 14. Processing Delays

Some transactions may be delayed due to payment verification, supplier processing, network interruptions, technical problems or third-party service issues.

A delay does not automatically mean that an order has failed.

Customers should contact support if a transaction remains unresolved or the expected delivery period has passed.

We will make reasonable efforts to investigate and provide an update.

## 15. Fraudulent or Abusive Refund Requests

We reserve the right to investigate refund requests that appear fraudulent, inconsistent or abusive.

Where reasonably necessary, we may request supporting information, temporarily suspend an order or restrict further transactions while an investigation is conducted.

Any action taken will remain subject to applicable law and customer rights.
